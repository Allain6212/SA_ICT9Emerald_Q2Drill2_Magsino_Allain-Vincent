The Four Hogwarts Houses

Every student belongs to one of four houses.


Gryffindor

Known for bravery, courage, and determination.

Slytherin

Known for ambition, resourcefulness, and leadership.

Ravenclaw

Known for intelligence, creativity, and wisdom.

Hufflepuff

Known for patience, loyalty, and hard work.

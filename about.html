About Hogwarts

Hogwarts is a school of magic located in the Scottish Highlands. It was founded over a thousand years ago by four powerful witches and wizards.



Our Founders

The four founders of Hogwarts are Godric Gryffindor, Helga Hufflepuff, Rowena Ravenclaw, and Salazar Slytherin. Each founder valued different qualities in students, which led to the creation of the four houses.


Life at Hogwarts


Students attend classes such as Potions, Charms, Transfiguration, and Defense Against the Dark Arts. They also participate in magical activities and Quidditch competitions.
